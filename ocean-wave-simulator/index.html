How it works
Waves
Eight Gerstner waves, from a long swell down to short chop, evaluated on the GPU for the surface and again on the CPU for physics, so barrels ride exactly what you see.
Light
An analytic sky (sun, haze, clouds, stars) reflected with Fresnel, a tight sun-glitter lobe, light scattered through the crests, and horizon fog. Barrels are lit by an environment map baked from the same sky.
Foam
Whitecaps come from the Jacobian of the wave displacement. Splashes and wakes are painted into a top-down foam texture that slowly fades.
Barrels
Rigid bodies with buoyancy sampled at fourteen points on the hull, water and air drag, orbital wave velocity, wind drift and capsule collisions.
